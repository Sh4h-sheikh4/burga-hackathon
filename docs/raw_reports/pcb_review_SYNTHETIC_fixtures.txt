### SYNTHETIC_bad_board
== ERRORS (9)
  - U1: VDD on net '+5V' - LSM6DS3TR-C max supply is 3.6 V (abs max 4.8 V). Use 3V3.
  - U1: VDD tied to Pi 5V - will damage the IMU.
  - U1: CS tied to GND -> SPI mode, I2C disabled, and no Pi SPI0 chip-select wired: interface is dead.
  - U1: SDO/SA0 (pin 1) floating - in I2C mode it is an input without pull-up; address undefined.
  - U1: SCL (pin 13) net '/SDA' != Pi GPIO3/SCL1 (header pin 5) net '/SCL'.
  - U1: SDA (pin 14) net '/SCL' != Pi GPIO2/SDA1 (header pin 3) net '/SDA'.
  - U1: SDx (pin 2) is floating (net 'None'). ST DS Table 2 / AN5130 sec 3.6: in Mode 1 SDx/SCx must be connected to VDDIO or GND and cannot be left floating (recommended: VDDIO). A schematic no-connect flag hides this from ERC.
  - U1: no decoupling capacitor between VDD net '+5V' and GND (datasheet: 100 nF).
  - U1: no decoupling capacitor between VDDIO net '+3V3' and GND (datasheet: 100 nF).
